## KNOWN RISKS

- #588 tracks a concrete fixture publication-order defect and the original
  CI's unresolved exact predicate. Passing retries alone do not fix that risk;
  #590 is merged; the original predicate remains historically unknown. This
  is not a demonstrated RUT semantic gap.
- Pinned-nginx evidence is version/digest-specific and does not establish
  general nginx.conf, process/resource, or network-equivalence support.
- URI normalization, trailing-slash routing, broader methods/framing, retries,
  reuse, pipeline expansion, TLS/H2, and wider timeout/buffering domains remain
  excluded unless separately evidenced.
- Parser acceptance, lowering output, and same-file differential evidence are
  separate gates; no one gate should be presented as completing the others.
